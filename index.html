ABOUT THE PROJECT

Art connects us to our past.

Indian Art Atlas is an educational project that brings together important locations in Indian art history. It uses an interactive map, short descriptions and historical context to show how art reflects the beliefs, creativity and traditions of different regions.

The decorative artwork on this website is illustrative. It is not a photograph of the original historical artworks.
